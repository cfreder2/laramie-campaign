A Supplement to The Frontier Index

Ivinson & Co. General Store

— Goods, Arms & Powder for Sale · Grand Avenue, Laramie City, W.T. · 1868 —
"If We Have Not Got It, You Have Not Yet Need of It"

Herein are set down the particulars of every implement of work and violence a man may purchase at our counter, with their effects rendered plain for the careful buyer. Prices in dollars (gold pieces). Damage and properties given for the use of those who keep ledgers of such things. For drink, smoke, and the comforts of vice, inquire at the saloon's bill of fare, not at this counter.

Winchester '66 Repeater

$48 · avg 5–6 dmg
Damage1d10 piercing
Range80 / 240 ft
Capacity15 shots
Two-handed. Repeating — fires every round (like a revolver, never single-shot). Misfire 1. Holds 15 — you will not empty it in any ordinary fight.
Why it costs near three times a Colt: twice the reach (240 ft) and a magazine deep enough to hold off a posse without running dry. It hits softer than the buffalo guns (1d10, not 1d12) — but they fire every other round while the Winchester fires every one. Rate and range, at a price.

Shot-Gun

Single-Barrel Shot-Gun

$14 · avg 7 dmg
Damage2d6 piercing
Area15-ft cone
Capacity1 shell
Cone, not aimed — every creature in a 15-ft cone makes a DC 13 Dexterity save: 2d6 on a fail, half on a success. No attack roll; no armor helps (you dodge shot, you don't tank it). Point-Blank — a target within 5 ft has disadvantage on the save. Two-handed. Reload 1 (Action). Misfire 1.
Close-up and area-wide — it sweeps a doorway, a hallway, a knot of men. Useless past fifteen feet. One shell, then you reload. The room-clearer.
Shot-Gun

Double-Barrel Shot-Gun

$26 · avg up to 14 dmg
Damage2d6 / barrel
Area15-ft cone
Capacity2 shells
One barrel — 15-ft cone, DC 13 Dex save, 2d6 (half on save). Both Barrels (one Action) — twice the shot: 4d6 in the cone, DC 15 Dex save (half), then both barrels are spent. Point-Blank disadvantage on the save. Two-handed. Reload 2 (Action). Misfire 1.
Twice the price for twice the shot — empty both barrels and the cone deals 4d6 to everyone in it. The catch: do it and you are holding an empty gun, reloading while the room reloads on you.

Tomahawk

$2 · avg 3–4 dmg
Damage1d6 slashing
Rangemelee · thrown 20/60
Light · Thrown · Brutal · Silent. Brutal — a melee hit dealing 10+ damage forces a Wound check (Con save DC 10 + half dmg), the same as a bullet. On a crit, roll one extra weapon die. Being Light, a warrior may wield two and make a bonus-action second strike.
Up close it is terror itself — it bites like a gunshot and a man with two of them is a storm of edges. Modest at a throw, fearsome in the hand. The plains warrior's argument.

Thrown · Versatile · Silent. The longest reach of any hurled weapon — flies farther than knife or hawk. Brace — ready it against a charging foe; if they close into melee you strike first and add +1d8.

Mounted

War Lance

$4 · avg 4–5, more on the charge
Damage1d8 piercing
Reach10 ft · Brutal
Reach · Brutal. Mounted Charge — if you move at least 20 ft on horseback in a straight line before the hit, add +2d8 damage. Brutal — a hit of 10+ forces a Wound check like a bullet. Unwieldy on foot (no bonus, hard to use in a clinch).
From the saddle at a gallop it is the deadliest weapon on the plains — a single charge can end a man. On foot it is an awkward stick. The horse is half the weapon.

Marvel

The Hesperus — Patent Self-Striking Pick

$225 · avg 7 dmg, twice
Damage2d6 piercing
Rangemelee
HeftTwo-handed · Heavy
Twice-Striking — when you take the Attack action, make one extra attack with it (the spring drives two strikes per swing). Clockwork (Wind 3) — runs 3 rounds, then must be wound (Bonus Action) before further use. Misfire 1 — on a natural 1 the works seize; a full Action clears it. Rock-Eater — chews stone, timber, and masonry at thrice the speed of any hand tool; advantage on Strength checks to dig, breach, or demolish.
Laramie's first auto-pick — a Gnome's clockwork wonder, dear as a team of horses. Two blows for every swing and it eats through rock like bread. But it must be wound, it can seize at the worst moment, and three rounds of its shaking in a close shaft is its own kind of danger (Con save DC 12 or a level of exhaustion).
