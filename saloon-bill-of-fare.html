The Bucket of Blood

Bill of Fare
— The Tap, The Bottle, and The Comfort of Vice —
(prices the same at any honest house on the street)

There is no free cup in Laramie: every drink carries a boon and a bane, and both apply the moment it goes down. Drink to warm the blood and steady the nerve — drink past that, and the bottle starts collecting what it lent.

Spirits

Whiskey — Good Rye

50¢ · the glass
Boon (always) — Deaden Pain — for 1 hour, ignore the attack-roll penalty of your first Wound; 1d4 temporary hit points; advantage on saves vs. being Frightened.
Bane (always) — disadvantage on Dexterity and Intelligence checks for 1 hour. +1 on the Cups track.
A shot before the duel quiets the wound and the fear with it — at the price of a wandering hand. Choose your moment.
Spirits

Rotgut Whiskey

15¢ · the glass
Boon (always) — Deaden Pain as the good rye, and 1d6 temporary hit points — more courage for less coin.
Bane (always) — DC 12 Constitution save or Poisoned for 1 hour. +2 on the Cups track.
Tarantula juice. Cheaper, braver, and twice as likely to put you under the table — or under the ground.
Tap

Beer / Lager

25¢ · the mug
Boon (always) — Liquid Courage — 1d4 temporary hit points; advantage on saves vs. being Frightened for 1 hour.
Bane (always) — Dulled — −1 to passive Perception and to Wisdom (Perception) checks for 1 hour. +1 on the Cups track.
Warms the blood and steadies the nerve — and is why the man at the next table heard nothing before the knife.
Pot

Coffee — Arbuckle's

10¢ · the cup
Boon (always) — for 1 hour: +2 Initiative, advantage on saves vs. sleep and fatigue, and ignore the effects of one level of exhaustion.
Bane (always) — The Jitters — for that hour: disadvantage on steady-hand Dexterity checks (Sleight of Hand, Thieves' Tools, fine work), you cannot use Steady the Bead (aim), and you cannot benefit from a nap on a short rest.
Quick, sharp, wide awake — and a hand too jittered to aim a long shot or pick a lock. (Not drink; does not advance the Cups track.)
Smoke

A Good Cigar

10¢ · the smoke
Boon (always) — Steady Nerves — for 10 minutes, advantage on your next saving throw against being Frightened.
Bane (always) — The Cloud — for 10 minutes, disadvantage on Stealth checks (the smell, the cough) and on Constitution checks to hold your breath or endure hard exertion.
A moment's calm wrapped in tobacco — and a trail of smoke that announces you to every nose in the dark. (Does not advance the Cups track.)
Temperance

Sarsaparilla

10¢ · the bottle
Boon (always) — Clear-Headed — you take on no drink-banes and add nothing to the Cups track; advantage on saves vs. being charmed or deceived for the hour. A sober mind in a room of loose ones.
Bane (always) — The Teetotaler — a man who won't drink is a man not trusted in a hard room: disadvantage on Charisma checks to win over rough company while you're visibly off the bottle.
The clear-eyed choice — and the one that marks you as a preacher, a Pinkerton, or a fool.

The Cups Track

Only spirits and beer advance the track — coffee, smoke, and sarsaparilla do not make a man drunk. Each alcoholic drink is one step (rotgut counts as two). The boons stand; the banes of drunkenness pile atop one another. A canny fighter stops at Warmed. A desperate one drinks past it on purpose.
